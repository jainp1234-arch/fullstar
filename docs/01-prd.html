Product Requirements · Draft v1.2

FullStar

Lessons from your own school book.

A home-learning app for children in Class 1–5. It turns a child's own school books into short animated lessons, practice and printable worksheets, with grade-level grammar and concepts ready from day one. Content is in Hindi and English; the app itself is in English.

Owner: Atul SinghStatus: For reviewLaunch scope: Class 3, NCERT/CBSE

1Why we are building this

Parents of young children want daily, structured study at home that follows the school syllabus. Today they juggle textbooks, YouTube videos and worksheets bought online, none of which tracks how the child is improving.

Goal: in about 20–30 minutes a day, a child learns a chapter, practises it, reads aloud, and the parent can see exactly where the child needs help.

2Who it is for

Child (6–10 years)

Uses the app on a tablet or phone. Needs big buttons, pictures, sound, read-aloud instructions and stars for fun. Cannot type much.

Parent

Signs up, uploads the child's books, prints worksheets and checks progress. Wants it to be safe and simple.

Admin / content team (internal)

Prepares lessons and question banks with Claude, checks them, and publishes them. Handles problem reports from parents.

4Adding content: whole book or chapter by chapter

Many schools hand out content one chapter at a time, so uploading a single chapter must be as easy as uploading a full book. Each subject has one book, built either way.

Option A · Upload the whole book

  • Upload one PDF, or photos of all the pages.
  • The app suggests where each chapter starts, using the contents page and chapter headings.
  • The parent checks the split, renames or merges chapters if needed, and saves.

Option B · Upload chapter by chapter

  • Pick the subject, then Add chapter.
  • Upload a PDF or page photos for that chapter only, and give it a name and number (e.g. "Ch 4 · पानी").
  • Add more chapters later as the school covers them. Chapters can be reordered, replaced or deleted.
RuleDetail
File types and limitsPDF up to 50 MB and 300 pages. JPG/PNG photos up to 10 MB each, 50 per upload. Photos can be taken with the device camera.
ChecksEvery upload is virus-scanned. If pages are unclear, the parent retakes only those pages.
What happens nextThe upload notifies our admin, who prepares the lesson and question bank (section 7). The parent gets an in-app message when each chapter is ready.
Replacing a bookAllowed; progress on the old book's chapters is removed after a warning.
PrivacyUploads are private to the family.

5App structure

The app opens on Who is learning?. Children only see kid mode. Parent mode is behind the parent PIN. Every kid-mode screen has a 🔊 button that reads the instructions aloud. Full screen-by-screen detail is in the App Flow document.

Kid mode · 3 tabs

  • Learn: subjects → chapter or topic → lesson, read-along or worksheet.
  • Practice: English, Hindi, Maths tables, EVS quiz, fix my mistakes.
  • Read: reading test, words to practise, reading level.

Parent mode · 4 tabs

  • Progress: This week, areas to work on, reading trend.
  • Books: upload a whole book or a chapter, preview new lessons, fix pages, replace a book.
  • Worksheets: create, print or share, view solutions, tick weak spots.
  • Settings: children, move to next class, PIN, time limit, sound, help, privacy.

6Key user journeys

Parent sets up (about 5 minutes)

  1. Signs up with email + one-time code and gives consent for the child.
  2. Sets the parent PIN and adds the child (Class 3).
  3. Uploads the books, or the first chapters, for each subject.
  4. Sets a daily time limit and hands the tablet to the child.

Child's session (20–30 minutes)

  1. Taps their picture and picks a subject.
  2. Watches a 10-minute lesson and answers the quiz questions.
  3. Does a practice set or tables challenge.
  4. Reads a passage aloud and gets stars and words to practise.

Parent prints a worksheet

  1. Picks a chapter or topic; the app shuffles questions.
  2. Prints or shares the PDF.
  3. Checks the child's work against the solutions and ticks weak spots.
  4. Weak spots show up in Areas to work on.

7How we create content

Our admin uses Claude in the Content Studio to prepare animated lessons and question banks, both for grade-level topics and for every chapter a family uploads. Each upload sends a notification to the admin so lessons are ready quickly.

CheckWhoWhat it does
1. Prepare and reviewAdminDrafts with Claude, edits, previews.
2. Content checkContent-check agentFlags content unsuitable for the child's age, facts that don't match the chapter, wrong answers, spelling or Hindi script errors, wrong difficulty, bias, and personal information. Critical flags block publishing.
3. Parent previewParent (recommended, not required)Watches new lessons for uploaded chapters and taps Looks good or Report a problem.

Children never talk to an AI and never see content that has not passed the admin review and the content check.

8Privacy & safety

  • Follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023: the parent gives consent at sign-up, we collect only the parent's email and the child's first name, age and class, there are no ads and no tracking.
  • Parents can request deletion of their data from Settings; our team completes it.
  • Reading recordings are processed on our own servers, never shared with outside companies, and deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Uploaded books are private to the family. Book text is used with Claude only to prepare that family's lessons; no child data is sent.
  • No chat and no contact with strangers. Parents can report a problem on any lesson or worksheet.
  • All messages stay inside the app.

11Not in the first version

  • Payments, free trial and subscriptions.
  • Hindi app language (the Hindi subject is included).
  • Computer subject; more than one book per subject; a book list to pick from.
  • Daily plan, streaks, bonus activities, parent assigning lessons.
  • Child PIN, second parent login, sibling comparison.
  • Mobile number sign-in by SMS.
  • SMS, WhatsApp or email notifications (email is used only for sign-in codes).
  • Self-service account deletion (handled as a request).
  • Native Android/iOS apps, pronunciation scoring, live tutors, chat, school accounts.
